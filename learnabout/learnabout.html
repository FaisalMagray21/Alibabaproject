Alibaba.com: Leading Global B2B E-commerce Platform

Alibaba.com is a leading global B2B (business-to-business) e-commerce platform that connects suppliers and buyers worldwide. Founded in 1999 by Jack Ma, it has grown to become one of the world's largest online trading platforms for small businesses.

Learn more on Wikipedia

Key Features of Alibaba.com:

  • Product Sourcing: Buyers can explore a vast array of products across various categories, including electronics, apparel, machinery, and more.
  • Supplier Verification: The platform offers tools to verify suppliers, ensuring reliability and trustworthiness in transactions.
  • Trade Assurance: Alibaba.com's Trade Assurance service protects orders and payments, providing security and confidence in transactions. Download on Google Play
  • Global Reach: With suppliers and buyers from over 240 countries and regions, Alibaba.com facilitates international trade and business expansion. Learn more on Wikipedia

Recent Developments:

  • AI Integration: In June 2024, Alibaba.com announced its commitment to scaling AI tools for micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs), aiming to enhance business efficiency and customer experiences. Learn more on Marketwatch
  • E-commerce Integration: In November 2024, Alibaba Group integrated its domestic Chinese and international e-commerce platforms into a single unit called the Alibaba E-commerce Business Group, consolidating platforms like Taobao, Tmall, AliExpress, Alibaba.com, 1688, and Xianyu.
